# SPRINT 03 — Governed Visualization

## Context

After establishing historical and simulation layers, the third sprint focused on visualization for the Academia domain. The intent was to present outputs without collapsing the separation between factual data and hypothetical scenarios.

## Goal

Create visualization rules and documentation that present historical and simulated data as distinct, governed outputs.

## Work Done

- Documented visualization boundaries aligned with constitutional rules.
- Defined how historical and simulated outputs are presented without merging them.
- Ensured traceability of visualization artifacts to their source layers.

## Pull Requests

- PR-005: Visualization governance and scope for Academia (placeholder).
- PR-006: Documentation updates for visual outputs and traceability (placeholder).

## Result

A governed visualization layer that preserves the separation of historical data and simulated scenarios.

## Non-Goals

- No modifications to historical or simulation data.
- No prescriptive directives or decisions.
- No dashboards.
