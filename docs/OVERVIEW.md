# Overview

## Purpose and Motivation

governanca-system exists to provide a governance-first framework for handling operational data with explicit traceability. The project was created to demonstrate how institutional constraints, data contracts, and auditability can be enforced without relying on opaque logic or implicit assumptions. Its objective is to keep the system structurally simple while remaining rigorously controlled by a single constitutional source of truth.

## Governance and Traceability Requirements

Governance is required to ensure that every artifact, rule, and analytical output is explainable and traceable. Traceability is the mechanism that validates compliance with the constitutional rules and ensures that no result exists outside a documented decision path. This makes the system suitable for environments where accountability, auditability, and repeatability are mandatory.

## Reuse of the Academia Domain

The Academia domain is intentionally reused across all projects to keep the domain vocabulary stable while different layers are demonstrated. Using the same domain avoids semantic drift, allows direct comparison of outputs across projects, and highlights how governance rules remain constant even as the system is applied to historical data, simulations, and visualization.

## Non-Recommendation Posture

governanca-system does not issue recommendations or decisions. It exposes structured facts, controlled analysis, and governed simulations without crossing into decision-making. This protects the separation between evidence and action, ensuring that the system remains an instrument for human decision-makers rather than an automated authority.
