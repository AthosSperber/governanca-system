# Interview Walkthrough

## Purpose

This document explains how to present the project in a 5–10 minute technical interview. It is a guided narrative for accuracy and restraint, not a pitch.

## 5–10 Minute Structure

### 1) Problem Addressed (Structural, Not Business)

- The project addresses a structural problem: how to define governance before any domain-specific intelligence or decision logic.
- It formalizes what is allowed, forbidden, and reviewable in a governance system, independent of a specific application.
- It treats the protocol as a contract, not a feature set.

### 2) What the System Intentionally Refuses to Do

- It does not make decisions or recommendations.
- It does not implement domain logic or business rules.
- It does not simulate outcomes or optimize results.
- It does not claim efficacy, performance, or superiority.

### 3) Why Governance Exists Before Intelligence

- Governance is defined first to constrain how intelligence could later be introduced without changing the rules.
- The Constitution and protocol establish boundaries, review signals, and non-goals before any domain-specific work.
- This ordering prevents hidden scope creep and protects the system from implicit promises.

### 4) How to Frame the Academia Domain

- Academia is a reference domain used to test the framework’s boundaries.
- It is a controlled example, not a commitment to that domain.
- The domain shows how Tasks, Actions, and Reports would be expressed without changing the governance layer.

### 5) How to Answer “Why Didn’t You Add Feature X?”

- The project is deliberately complete at the governance level.
- Adding a feature would imply a new domain commitment or a change in protocol scope.
- The correct response is: “This repository defines governance constraints. Feature work belongs to a separate, explicitly scoped domain project.”

## Optional Closing (If Time Allows)

- Emphasize that the repository is documentation-driven and bounded by a Constitution.
- Confirm that the project’s value is precision, not expansion.

## What to Avoid

- Do not claim market relevance, adoption, or superiority.
- Do not promise roadmap items or future capabilities.
- Do not present the framework as a product.
