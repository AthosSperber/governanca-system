# Governance Guarantees

This document defines what the governance framework guarantees and what it explicitly does not guarantee. It is normative and describes structural properties only.

## Guarantees

### Traceability Guarantees

- Every governed change is expected to be attributable to a documented task, action, or report.
- Governance artifacts are recorded as documents, not implicit behavior.
- Links between decisions, changes, and documentation remain inspectable.

### Separation Guarantees

- Historical records are separated from simulations and visualizations.
- Domain implementations do not collapse epistemic layers.
- Governance documentation does not alter runtime behavior.

### Audit Guarantees

- Governance scope and non-goals are explicitly documented.
- Changes are reviewable without executing the system.
- Governance artifacts can be inspected for alignment with the Constitution.

### Semantic Guarantees

- Protocol semantics remain stable unless explicitly governed.
- Documents define intent, boundaries, and alignment without adding logic.
- Governance language describes structure rather than outcomes.

### Evolution Guarantees

- Governance evolves through documented sprints.
- Changes are anchored in declared goals and non-goals.
- The Constitution remains the source of truth and is not overridden by sprint work.

## Non-Guarantees

The framework explicitly does not guarantee:

- Decision correctness or decision quality.
- Optimization, efficiency, or performance.
- Business outcomes or institutional success.
- Prediction accuracy or forecasting validity.
- Recommendation validity or prescriptive guidance.
- Completeness of domain coverage.
- Enforcement of compliance outside documented review processes.
- Automatic conflict resolution between stakeholders.
